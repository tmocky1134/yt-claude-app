youtube-claude

youtube-claude is a private, single-user tool. It lets the owner of this site use an AI assistant (Claude Code) to work with their own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API v3.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's channel, videos, playlists and comments.
  • On the owner's instruction, updates video details, playlists and comments on the owner's channel.

The app is not offered to the public. It only accesses the YouTube account of its owner, who authorised it via Google's OAuth consent screen.
